# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Health Matters is a project about helping people manage something deeply
personal: their health information. We want everyone who contributes to feel
respected, safe, and welcome, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or
background.

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Being kind, patient, and respectful toward others.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people it is meant to serve.
- Acknowledging mistakes, taking responsibility, and learning from them.
- Protecting the privacy of others, including using only synthetic data in all
  project work.
- Being accurate and honest about what the project can and cannot do.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission.
- Sharing real medical records or health information about any person in
  issues, pull requests, discussions, screenshots, or anywhere else in the
  project.
- Knowingly misrepresenting the project's capabilities, for example claiming
  regulatory compliance or clinical validity that the project does not have.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Health and safety

This project is a prototype and is not medical advice. Contributors and
participants should not use project spaces to give personal medical advice or
to ask others to interpret their own medical records. If you need medical help,
please contact a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency services.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel used for project
communication. It also applies when an individual is representing the project
in public, for example at a hackathon, demo, or presentation.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior. They will take fair and appropriate action in response
to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow
this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason for moderation decisions when
appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the
project maintainers at **[INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]**.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers
will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reported the incident.
Please do not include real patient data in a report.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

1. **Correction.** For a minor or first-time issue. A private message
   explaining what happened and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public
   apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a more serious violation or repeated behavior. A formal
   warning with consequences for continued behavior, which may include a
   temporary restriction on interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation or sustained inappropriate
   behavior. A temporary ban from all interaction with the project for a set
   period.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual,
   or aggression toward others. A permanent ban from the project.

## Conflicts of interest

If a report involves a maintainer, that maintainer will not take part in
reviewing or deciding on it.

## Acknowledgment

This Code of Conduct was written for the Health Matters project and draws on
widely shared open source community standards.
